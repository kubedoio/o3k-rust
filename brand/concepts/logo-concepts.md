# O3K logo — concept explorations and decision

Working session, 2026-09-09. Three concepts were developed as geometry sketches,
scored, and one was selected. Scores: 1–5 per criterion (5 = strongest).

## Concept A — "Bounded Growth" (grid panel + escaping module)

A flat 3×3 substrate grid drawn as light cell outlines. Three cells are active
(filled). Two sit inside the grid; the third has crossed the top-right grid
boundary and sits outside it, aligned to the same module rhythm. The vacated
slot remains visible as an empty outline.

Meaning: small kernel (few active cells), capability building blocks (modules),
structured substrate (grid), deterministic growth (the module leaves the panel
along the grid axis, not randomly). Edge → datacenter: the same block language
works at one-cell scale and at a field of hundreds.

At 16 px: the fine grid recedes but the three filled modules remain a distinct
stair-step signature. At ≥24 px the substrate is legible.

## Concept B — "Kernel core" (central block + ring)

One solid central rounded square surrounded by an eight-cell 3×3 outline grid; two filled
cells adjacent; one filled cell outside the boundary. No empty-slot story.

Meaning: kernel at the center of the substrate.

Weakness: the centered solid reads as a generic "hub" mark (very common in
platform branding); the center-weighted composition is less distinctive at
16 px (collapses to a blob with faint ring); the "growth/no-replatforming"
story is weaker — nothing visibly leaves the system boundary.

## Concept C — "Scale strata" (three widening rows of blocks)

Three horizontal rows of blocks of increasing width (1 block, 2 blocks, 3
blocks), suggesting scale rungs from edge to datacenter.

Meaning: growth as widening tiers.

Weakness: reads as a bar chart / hamburger menu at small sizes; vertical
stacking suggests hierarchy rather than one continuous substrate; weakest
connection to the "same substrate grows outward" product truth; poor
icon-family generator.

## Decision

**Concept A — "Bounded Growth"** is the canonical O3K mark.

Why it won:

1. It is the only concept where growth is *visible in the geometry itself*:
   one module has crossed the panel boundary while staying on the grid rhythm —
   the exact product claim (scale without replatforming) expressed as shape.
2. The empty slot it leaves behind gives the mark a second, quieter story:
   capacity is modular; blocks join and the substrate stays structured.
3. It degrades gracefully: at 16 px the outline grid recedes and three solid
   modules remain, still a recognizable signature.
4. The cell/grid/module grammar is a direct generator for the infrastructure
   glyph family Araf will consume, keeping O3K (substrate) and Araf (interface)
   on one visual grammar.
5. It contains no cloud silhouette, no wheel, no hexagon, no orbit, no cube.

Rejected concepts B and C remain documented here as explorations and must not
be used as alternate official marks.
